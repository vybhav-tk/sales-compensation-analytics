# Excel Portfolio Model

This folder contains the primary Excel deliverable for the **Sales Compensation & Incentive Analytics** portfolio project.

## Main File

`sales_compensation_portfolio_model.xlsx`

The workbook consolidates the end-to-end compensation process into one interview- and portfolio-ready model.

## Workbook Flow

The workbook is organized to follow the business process:

1. Dashboard
2. Model Guide
3. Compensation Rules
4. Quota Coverage
5. Sales Crediting
6. Monthly Performance and Commission
7. Q3 Technology Services SPIFF
8. Compensation Controls
9. Commission Forecasting
10. Plan Effectiveness
11. Payout Traceability
12. Business Insights
13. Management Actions

## What the Workbook Demonstrates

The model shows how a Sales Compensation Analyst can translate:

**sales transactions → eligible credited sales → effective quota → attainment → commission → incentive awards → controls → approval → forecasting → management insight**

Key capabilities demonstrated include:

- effective quota calculation
- employee lifecycle proration
- transaction-level compensation crediting
- quota attainment
- marginal commission tiers
- accelerator calculations
- SPIFF administration
- exception management
- payroll reconciliation
- quota coverage analysis
- compensation-expense forecasting
- plan-effectiveness analysis
- management reporting

## Control Principle

A major design principle is:

> **Calculated compensation is not automatically approved compensation.**

The workbook separates working calculations from control status and approval status so mathematically correct results can still be blocked when required inputs or eligibility conditions are invalid.

## Synthetic Data Disclaimer

All employee, sales, quota, incentive, and compensation data in this workbook is synthetic.

The compensation rules were created specifically for this portfolio project and do not represent any real employer's compensation plan or payroll process.

## Suggested Review Path

For a quick portfolio review:

1. Open the Dashboard.
2. Review the Rules sheet.
3. Follow Quota Coverage → Sales Credit → Performance & Commission.
4. Review Controls and Payroll reconciliation.
5. Review Forecasting and Effectiveness.
6. Finish with Traceability and Business Insights.

The workbook is intended to demonstrate analytical reasoning, controls, auditability, and stakeholder communication rather than only formula construction.
