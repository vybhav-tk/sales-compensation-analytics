# Interview Walkthrough

## 60–90 Second Project Summary

This project simulates an end-to-end Sales Compensation Analyst workflow. I started with the business problem rather than the tools: management needs incentive payouts that are accurate, explainable, auditable, and aligned with documented plan rules. I then structured synthetic participant, performance, quota, and compensation-plan data; defined the data grain and relationships; validated the inputs; calculated attainment and incentive earnings; built QA and reconciliation controls; analyzed payout and performance patterns; and designed scenario testing to show the cost impact of plan changes. The final visualization layer is being built in Power BI.

## What the Project Demonstrates

- Understanding of quota attainment and incentive-calculation concepts
- Ability to translate business rules into structured calculations
- Data validation and reconciliation discipline
- Excel and SQL analytical workflow
- Exception and outlier analysis
- Scenario modeling
- Management-oriented communication
- Power BI reporting design

## Why Synthetic Data Was Used

The project is intentionally built with synthetic data so it can demonstrate realistic compensation analytics without exposing confidential employee, payroll, sales, quota, or compensation-plan information.

## Strong Interview Framing

The most important point is that the project is not just a commission calculator. It models the responsibilities around the calculation: data integrity, rule interpretation, auditability, reconciliation, exception handling, analysis, and stakeholder communication.
